Please read this form, ask any questions, and sign only if you agree. Saying no has no consequences.

## What we will record

Tick everything you agree to.

- [ ] **Lip movement data.** While you silently mouth phrases in front of a laptop webcam, the software stores the positions of 42 points around your lips and eyes in each frame. **No images or video are stored** unless you also tick the next box.
- [ ] **Video (optional).** Short video clips of my face, used only for the poster or presentation images.
- [ ] **Voice sample (optional).** A 30–60 second recording of my speaking voice, used to make a synthetic copy of my voice (voice cloning) that can say only the project's fixed phrase list.

## How the data is used

- Only to train and test the SilentSpeech prototype, and to report results (for example accuracy numbers and charts) in our competition presentation and poster. Results are never linked to your name.
- The data stays on the team's laptops. It is not uploaded to GitHub or shared with anyone outside the team.
- A synthetic version of your voice is used only inside SilentSpeech, is clearly labelled as AI-generated, and is never used to say anything other than the fixed phrase list.
- If the optional LLM layer is used, it receives only phrase text, never your images, video or lip data.

## Your rights

- You can stop at any time during recording.
- You can ask us to delete all your data, including any voice recordings and cloned audio, at any time by contacting the person above. We will delete it within 7 days and confirm.
- The data will be deleted at the end of the project (`[date]`) unless you agree in writing to keep it longer.

## Medical note

SilentSpeech is a research prototype, not a medical device. Taking part is not medical treatment.
